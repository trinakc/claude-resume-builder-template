INTERVIEW QUESTION BANK  [EXAMPLE — REPLACE WITH YOUR OWN]
===========================================================
Question patterns encountered across past interviews, organized by
category. This is a reference of QUESTION TYPES, not answers — pair with
story-bank.txt when prepping for a specific role. Used to seed JD-tailored
question generation: match likely questions against this bank first, only
generate genuinely new ones where the JD covers ground this bank doesn't.

>>> HOW TO USE THIS FILE <<<
The categories below are a generic starting set. Add real questions verbatim
after each interview, under the closest category, or start a new category
when none fits. Parenthetical notes after a question record what the
interviewer was actually probing for, which is often more useful than the
question wording itself.

PEOPLE LEADERSHIP & COACHING
-------------------------------
- "How do you approach coaching someone whose technical background is very
  different from your own?"
- "Tell me about a time you had to give difficult feedback." (does the
  answer include what changed afterwards?)
- "How do you keep a distributed team cohesive across time zones?"

DELIVERY & PRIORITISATION
----------------------------
- "Tell me about a time you had to choose between scope, date, and quality."
- "How do you handle a commitment the team is clearly going to miss?"
- "What does your first 90 days look like in this role?"

TECHNICAL DEPTH
------------------
- "Walk me through a complex technical project you delivered."
- "What is a technical decision you got wrong, and what did you do about it?"
- "How hands-on are you now, and how hands-on do you want to be?"

QUALITY, RISK & INCIDENTS
----------------------------
- "How do you decide whether something is ready to release?"
- "Tell me about an incident you were involved in." (looking for the review
  and the systemic fix, not just the fix)
- "How do you balance test coverage against delivery speed?"

STAKEHOLDERS & CONFLICT
--------------------------
- "Describe a disagreement with a peer and how it resolved."
- "How do you say no to a stakeholder?"

MOTIVATION & FIT
-------------------
- "Why this role, and why now?"
- "What kind of environment do you do your best work in?"
- "What questions do you have for us?" (prepare three, role-specific)
