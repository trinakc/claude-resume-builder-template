Engineering Manager, Developer Platform
Northgate Labs — Remote (US / EU)

About the role
We are looking for an Engineering Manager to lead our Developer Platform
team. The platform team's customers are our own engineers: you will own the
build, CI/CD, and deployment tooling that every product team depends on.

What you will do
- Lead, coach, and grow a team of 5 to 8 platform engineers across time zones
- Own the reliability of the internal deployment pipeline and the release
  process that sits on top of it
- Partner with product engineering leads to reduce delivery friction and
  shorten feedback loops
- Drive incremental migration of legacy build infrastructure without
  disrupting product delivery
- Set and hold quality standards, including release go/no-go decisions

What we are looking for
- Experience managing software engineers, including hiring and development
- Hands-on background in CI/CD, build systems, or infrastructure automation
- A track record of improving developer experience with evidence of impact
- Comfort making judgement calls under delivery pressure
- Clear communication with both engineers and senior stakeholders

Nice to have
- Experience with cloud cost management
- Experience running incident review processes
